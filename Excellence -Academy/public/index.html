About Excellence Academy

A better learning experience begins with the right guidance.

Excellence Academy is an education-focused learning destination designed to help students strengthen their academic foundations, explore their interests and approach their studies with confidence.

We believe effective education combines clear explanations, regular practice, constructive feedback and a supportive relationship between students, educators and parents.

  • ✓ A structured and organised approach to learning
  • ✓ Opportunities to ask questions and clear doubts
  • ✓ Practice, revision and exam preparation
  • ✓ A focus on confidence and independent thinking

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions? Start here.

Find answers to common questions about courses, admissions and learning support at Excellence Academy.

Ask Us a Question ↗
Which classes and subjects are available?
We aim to provide academic coaching, Mathematics and Science support, board exam preparation, entrance exam guidance and revision support. Please contact us to confirm the classes and subjects currently offered.
How can I enquire about admission?
Complete the enquiry form on this page with your contact details, the student's class and the course of interest. Our actual contact details must be configured before the form can send enquiries.
Do you provide board exam preparation?
Board-oriented preparation is one of the learning services featured on this website. Ask us about the relevant board, syllabus, subjects, revision schedule and available batches.
Are online classes available?
Online availability has not yet been confirmed. Contact the academy to check whether online, in-person or hybrid learning options are offered.
How are course fees decided?
Fees, payment schedules and any additional costs should be confirmed directly with the academy. Contact us for current pricing before enrolling.
Can parents discuss a student's learning needs?
Yes, parents can use the enquiry form to request a discussion about the student's subjects, current challenges and learning goals. Meeting availability should be confirmed with the academy.
What should a student bring to class?
In general, students should bring their relevant textbooks, notebooks, stationery and any assigned practice work. Confirm any additional requirements with the academy before the first class.
How can I check class timings and availability?
Submit an enquiry specifying the student's class and preferred course. The academy can confirm the actual timetable, available places and enrolment process.
